- Title: How the heart pumps blood
- Label (always shown, verbatim): `[diagram, made by Mimir, simplified]`
- Intro (always shown): The heart is two pumps side by side. The right side sends blood
  to the lungs; the left side sends it to the body. Step through the path of the blood.
- Step 1: Right atrium. Deoxygenated blood from the body returns through the superior
  and inferior venae cavae into the right atrium.
- Step 2: Right ventricle. Blood passes the tricuspid valve into the right ventricle.
- Step 3: Pulmonary trunk. The right ventricle contracts; blood passes the pulmonary
  valve into the pulmonary trunk, which carries it to the lungs to pick up oxygen.
- Step 4: Left atrium. Oxygenated blood returns from the lungs through the pulmonary
  veins into the left atrium.
- Step 5: Left ventricle. Blood passes the mitral (bicuspid) valve into the left
  ventricle, whose wall is the thickest of the four chambers.
- Step 6: Aorta. The left ventricle contracts; blood passes the aortic valve into the
  aorta and out to the body.
- Caption (always shown): Simplified frontal section: the patient's right is on your
  left. Both atria contract together, then both ventricles; the steps follow the blood
  along one path.
